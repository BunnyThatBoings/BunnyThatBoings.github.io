Efficient Demon List (v2.4)

Starting at 1%, for every percentage you get on a randomly chosen level, you must get increasingly higher percentages on more random levels until you either give up or get 100%.


Coded by urcool626

Completions
Overrated
Fairly Placed
Underrated

The EDL is made by CircularCyclone, ThePastaNebula, & urcool626

EDL list mods: CircularCyclone, oish, Lani, & lanx

The EDL was made because we didn't like the arbitrary restrictions that Pointercrate and the Global Demonlist put on levels, as their rate status (which is decided by RobTop, who has basically no connection to modern top play). Additionally, we didn't agree with the methodology of the other lists, as they take too long to place levels, don't listen to actual top player feedback, and cater too much to players who are opinion lying.
